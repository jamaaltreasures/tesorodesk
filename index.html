SYS / 10 · FAQ

06 answers

Good questions.

Short answers about where Tesoro Desk is today. Ask us anything else in the beta section.

01What is Tesoro Desk?

Tesoro Desk lets you see and control your own Mac from your iPhone. Tesoro Host is a small Mac app that shares your screen with your iPhone over your private network.

02Can I use it today?

Not yet. Tesoro Desk is in testing. Join the waitlist and we will write when there is something to try. The beta link is coming soon.

03What do I need?

A Mac running Tesoro Host and an iPhone. Apple Watch is optional. The exact system versions are coming soon.

04Does it work on Apple Watch?

You can see your Mac on Apple Watch and zoom in. The picture is not sharp yet, and we are working on it.

05Where do I get the Mac app?

Tesoro Host is not ready to download yet. When it is, the download will be on this site, and the waitlist will hear first.

06How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Use the forms in the beta section. Every report goes to the team building Tesoro Desk, and news shows up in the live updates.
